Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

TubeIQ provides YouTube channel analytics and insights. This Privacy Policy explains what data we access and how we use it.

Google User Data and YouTube API Data

When you sign in with Google, TubeIQ requests read-only access to your YouTube account using the youtube.readonly scope.

TubeIQ uses the YouTube Data API to read channel information and channel statistics (for example, subscriber count, view count, video count, and public video metadata) so we can show analytics dashboards and recommendations.

TubeIQ does not create, edit, upload, delete, or otherwise modify your YouTube content.

Data Sharing

TubeIQ does not sell or rent Google User Data. Public channel metadata and prompts that you submit to the optional Gemini assistant are sent to Google Gemini to generate AI suggestions. Do not submit confidential information to chat.

When you sign in, Google Identity Services and the YouTube Data API process your authentication and read-only channel access. Vercel hosts the site's backend API routes. TubeIQ does not request permission to modify your channel.

Data Retention

The current open-source application does not configure its own persistent analytics database. Its dashboard state and OAuth access token are held in browser memory while the page is open. Public API responses can be temporarily cached by the hosting infrastructure. Hosting and API providers may process request logs under their own policies.

To revoke read-only YouTube authorization, remove TubeIQ's access in your Google Account's third-party connections and sign out from the app.

Content Studio / Spreadsheet Uploads

You may type or paste your content calendar as text, copied cells, a table or a freeform message, or optionally upload an Excel/CSV file. Uploaded files and pasted structured tables are parsed in your browser. When you explicitly click Understand with AI, your freeform message is transmitted through our server to Google's Gemini API so it can be converted into calendar rows for your approval. When you separately click Review all with AI, the selected titles, dates, optional content notes and sampled public YouTube channel information are transmitted to Gemini for editorial suggestions. TubeIQ does not automatically publish or alter any YouTube videos or your original source file.

Original Excel files are not uploaded by the Studio as files to TubeIQ servers. The optional Save on this device action stores a copy of the reviewed rows, titles and selected channel context in this browser's local storage, which can be cleared by the app's Clear local draft action or by clearing browser site data. An imported channel snapshot may be stored in temporary session storage when navigating between the dashboard and Content Studio. Avoid importing sensitive records on shared devices.

The exported workbook includes a separate review worksheet where supported; exporting may not preserve every advanced Excel feature or formatting rule. Keep your untouched original workbook as a backup.

Security

We apply reasonable safeguards to protect data processed by TubeIQ. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Third-Party Services

TubeIQ uses Google Identity Services, YouTube Data API, and optional Google Gemini API. The application is deployed using Vercel. Your use of these services is also subject to their respective policies and terms.

Public YouTube counts may be rounded, delayed, hidden, or omitted by the API. TubeIQ does not infer private Studio measures such as revenue, CTR, watch time or retention.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project maintainer via GitHub Issues. Do not post private credentials in public issues.
